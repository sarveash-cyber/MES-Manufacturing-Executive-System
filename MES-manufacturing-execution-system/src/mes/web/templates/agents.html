02Meet the crew

Each specialist owns one slice of the plant's physics — and one term of the money equation.

🎛️

The Orchestrator

Lead agent · claude
"I don't do the digging. I decide where to dig."
  • Parses your question and checks what data exists (get_data_overview)
  • Answers simple lookups itself; fans out hard questions to the specialists
  • Merges findings into one consultant-grade answer with dollars and recommendations
Answer = Σ specialist findings, ranked by $ impact
📊

KPI Analyst

Subagent
"A trend line never lies. People who read them badly do."
  • Tracks OEE and its three gears across machines, products, months
  • Flags anomalies: the machine whose availability quietly erodes every month
  • Benchmarks: world-class ≈ 85%, typical ≈ 60%
OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality  ·  TEEP = OEE × Utilization
🕵️

Downtime Investigator

Deck role: Diagnosis Agent
"80% of your downtime hides behind 20% of your excuses."
  • Builds the downtime Pareto by reason code, category, machine
  • Separates breakdowns vs changeovers vs minor stops (the Six Big Losses)
  • Distinguishes chronic offenders from growing failures
Availability = (planned − downtime) / planned — this agent shrinks the red term
💰

Cost-Leakage Hunter

Subagent
"Minutes are cute. Show me the dollars."
  • Prices every loss: downtime × hourly rate, scrap × (material + machine time)
  • Ranks the leaks plant-wide and per machine/product/month
  • Estimates the annualized saving of halving each leak — your focus list
Bleed = Σ (loss minutes / 60) × $rate + scrap × ($material + cycle·$rate)
📋

Schedule Auditor

Deck role: Planner Agent
"Your plan said Tuesday. Reality filed for an extension."
  • Compares scheduled vs actual start and finish for every work order
  • Computes on-time %, order fulfilment (OTD), start delay, overrun
  • Traces slippage to its physical cause via the other agents' domains
Adherence = on-time orders / all orders  ·  overrun = actual − scheduled finish
🔧

Maintenance Strategist

Deck role: Diagnosis Agent
"Machines whisper before they scream. I speak fluent whisper."
  • Computes MTBF and MTTR per machine from the downtime log
  • Separates frequent-but-quick failures from rare-but-long ones
  • Prices preventive maintenance against avoided downtime cost
MTBF = run hours / breakdowns  ·  MTTR = Σ repair min / breakdowns
🧪

Quality Warden

Deck role: Diagnosis Agent
"Every scrapped part is paid for three times. I keep the receipts."
  • Watches FPY, scrap rate, ppm and the cost of poor quality
  • Ties defects to machines, products and operators
  • Ranks quality fixes by dollar payback
FPY = (units − scrap − rework) / units  ·  COPQ = scrap × ($mat + t·$rate)
📦

Material Controller

Deck role: Material Agent
"Your inventory is cash wearing a hi-vis vest. Some of it is asleep."
  • Tracks inventory turns, days of cover and carrying cost per product
  • Flags overstock (gears napping on racks) and starvation risk
  • Quantifies the saving of right-sizing stock levels
Turns = COGS / avg inventory  ·  DOI = 365 / turns
⚡

Energy Optimizer

Deck role: Energy Agent
"Watts that don't make parts are just expensive heat."
  • Meters kWh per good unit and energy spend per machine
  • Separates productive draw from idle draw during downtime
  • Estimates the annual saving of fixing the biggest energy hog
Energy efficiency = kWh / good unit  ·  spend = kWh × tariff
🧑‍💼

Sales Engineer

Advisor pitch writer
"You bring the numbers. I bring the 'and that's why you should sign'."
  • Turns Sales Advisor selections into executive pitch copy
  • Grounded only in the numbers it's given — never re-derives, never invents
  • Ends every pitch with the 3-week Money-Bleed Audit call to action
Pitch = headline $ + top levers × agent story + call to action
🧑‍🏭

Manufacturing SME

Chat co-pilot · web-enabled
"Ask me why, how or when. I'll bring receipts — from your data or the web."
  • Lives in the 🧑‍🏭 drawer on every page; multi-turn, page-aware
  • Numbers only from the KPI tools or cited web sources (strict grounding)
  • Pivots on evidence: follows your redirects, but shows the data when it disagrees
Answer = KPI tools first → cited web second → "I'd need to verify" never a guess
🧮

The KPI Engine

Not an agent · pure math
"I have no opinions. That is my entire value proposition."
  • Deterministic pandas pipeline over the MES schema — same input, same answer
  • Sums time buckets first, then takes ratios (never averages percentages)
  • The agents' only source of numbers, exposed as tools
window = run time + downtime  ·  ideal = cycle × units — identities enforced by tests

03Anatomy of one question

"Where am I bleeding money?" — what actually happens in the ~30 seconds that follow.

Orchestrator calls get_data_overview: 6 machines, 5 products, 12 months, ~4,000 work orders. Now it knows the battlefield.
It dispatches the Cost-Leakage Hunter (rank the $), the Downtime Investigator (explain the biggest time loss), and the KPI Analyst (find the trend) — in parallel.
Each specialist calls its KPI tools; the engine computes from SQLite; raw tables flow back. No number is ever made up — if the database is empty, the agents say so.
The orchestrator reconciles the findings: "CNC-01's bearing failures cost $X and are growing 20%/month; PACK-01's speed loss quietly burns $Y; ASM-01 scraps $Z of valve bodies."
You get one answer: headline, evidence, and the 2–3 fixes with the biggest payback. The agents return to standby. They do not unionize.

3½Configurable brains

Every agent's model is a setting, not a hard-code — swap or upgrade whenever a better brain ships.

Where
config/agents.yaml at the project root
What you can change
Per-agent model (sonnet / opus / haiku / inherit), enabled flag, orchestrator model & turn budget
How to apply
Edit the file, restart the server. No code changes. The chips on this page show the live config.
Why it matters
Models improve monthly; your MES logic shouldn't be married to one. The deck calls this the LLM Agent Framework layer — swappable by design.

04How agents enhance the formulas

The formulas are static physics; the agents make them actionable.

Formula alone
OEE = 61.3%. Interesting. Now what?
+ KPI Analyst
"OEE fell 6 points since March, entirely from Availability, entirely on CNC-01."
+ Downtime Investigator
"CNC-01's loss is one reason code: BRG-FAIL — spindle bearings, worsening monthly."
+ Cost-Leakage Hunter
"That bearing costs you ~$8k/month. A $15k spindle overhaul pays back in under 8 weeks."
+ Schedule Auditor
"It also explains why 40% of CNC-01's orders finish late — fix one thing, heal two KPIs."
= The point
From a percentage to a purchase order. That's the enhancement. 🔧
